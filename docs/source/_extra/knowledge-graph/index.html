Castelnuovo di Porto knowledge graph

The masonry aggregate of Castelnuovo di Porto described with the Historic Survey (HSV), Historic Structure (HSTO), Historic Masonry (HMO) and Failure Mechanism (FMO) ontologies: the survey and its documents, the façades and their walls, the mechanical properties of the masonry derived by a reasoner from the observed construction features, and the behaviour of the walls. Everything on this page is read from the graph itself.

Interactive graph

Drag to move, use + and − to zoom, click a node to see what the graph says about it. Photographs (shown as small pictures) and the HMO internals (quality index, properties, pattern entities) are hidden at first to keep the view readable; switch them on below.

Select a node to see its classes, values and relations.

BIM model

The BIM model of the aggregate, with every masonry element coloured by its masonry type and every other element, in muted tones, by its material. The BIM model and the graph are linked by the material name, as in the conversion to the numerical model: an element whose material is a masonry type of the graph is an element of that type, and any other material is a mat:Material of the graph, with the properties of the IFC material. Drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, use + and − to zoom, click an element to see it in the graph.

Click an element to see its IFC class, GlobalId and materials, and the masonry type it belongs to.

Façades and their masonry

Each façade is made of one masonry wall. Quality indices and properties were derived by Pellet from the HMO rules, the behaviour in each direction and the expected mechanisms from the FMO rules; the mass density is asserted, since HMO has no rule for it.

Masonry types

The pattern entities are the HMO constants the rules score; unit dimensions are entered as a representative length range for the size category recorded in the survey.

Derived and measured properties

Properties derived by the reasoner for the masonry as classified and for its variants (hypothetical walls with the uncertain parameters changed), next to the measured values recorded in the graph with their source and basis.

Documents and provenance
