Castelnuovo di Porto knowledge graph

The masonry aggregate of Castelnuovo di Porto described with the Historic Survey (HSV), Historic Structure (HSTO) and Historic Masonry (HMO) ontologies: the survey and its documents, the façades and their walls, and the mechanical properties of the masonry derived by a reasoner from the observed construction features. Everything on this page is read from the graph itself.

Interactive graph

Drag to move, use + and − to zoom, click a node to see what the graph says about it. Photographs and the HMO internals (quality index, properties, pattern entities) are hidden at first to keep the view readable; switch them on below.

Select a node to see its classes, values and relations.

Façades and their masonry

Each façade is made of one masonry wall. Quality indices and properties were derived by Pellet from the HMO rules; the mass density is asserted, since HMO has no rule for it.

Masonry types

The pattern entities are the HMO constants the rules score; unit dimensions are entered as a representative length range for the size category recorded in the survey.

Documents and provenance

Documents other than the photographs (listed per façade above), with the activity that produced them, what they derive from and who acquired, processed or authored them.
